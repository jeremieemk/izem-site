Hey, I'm Jérémie Kerouanton, aka iZem, a French beatmaker and producer.
I wanted to be a guitar player but I was too clumsy, plus I hate guitar solos.
Instead, I discovered I was much better at cutting and pasting sounds on a computer.

I left France at 25 and kept moving: Spain, Brazil, Argentina, Ireland, Portugal.
I made music with the people I met along the way (Mayra Andrade, Guts, Sara Tavares...); you can hear some of it on [spotify](https://open.spotify.com/artist/2dJpwMtmOBr9ad4D7vRf2r) / [soundcloud](https://soundcloud.com/izemsounds) / [youtube](https://www.youtube.com/@izem1286).

These days I'm working on my drum sequencing and sound design skills.
Also I want to produce and write more songs in Spanish.

If you want to make something together, or just talk music, feel free to DM me [here](https://www.instagram.com/izem__music/).
